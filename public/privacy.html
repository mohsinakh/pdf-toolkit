Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PDF Toolkit ("we", "our", "the website") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Your files stay on your device

All file processing (merging, splitting, compressing, converting PDFs and images) happens entirely in your web browser using your own device's processing power. We do not upload, store, transmit, or have access to any of the files you process. Nothing you work with ever leaves your device.

2. No personal data collection

We do not require an account, do not collect your name, email, or any personal information to use the tools.

3. Advertising

This website may display advertising. Third-party ad providers (such as Google AdSense or similar networks) may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's partner sites policy. You may opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookies and analytics

We may use basic analytics (such as privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics) to understand how many visitors use the tools. If advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data retention

Since files are not transmitted to us, we do not retain any of your files or personal data. Any minimal technical logs (such as page views) are kept only as long as needed for site operation and security.

6. Children's privacy

The website is a general-purpose tool and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us by email.
